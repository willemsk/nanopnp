#### 8.2.21 Phase 4 amended: the user-testing findings ruled, agreed 29 October 2026

Rulings by the author on `docs/user-testing/findings.md`, UT-01 to UT-12, taken at `/np-plan wp 56` on
29 October 2026 in place of a separate second `/np-plan amend 4` (§8.2.7 G7). The author's instruction
was to take each finding that is valid, to address it at its cause, and to generalise a finding's
principle where it has one. Four questions were put through `AskUserQuestion`; the author took the
recommendation each time. W1 is the first answer, W7 the second, W3 the third and W8 the fourth; W2,
W4, W5 and W6 are the generalisations that the instruction asked for, drafted in the same round. The
letter series continues at W.

| # | Decision | Consequence | Clause changed, and when |
|---|---|---|---|
| W1 | **The findings are ruled at WP56's plan, and the fix packages are two**: WP56, the command line (UT-01 to UT-09, UT-11, UT-12, and the half of UT-10 that the shell uses), and WP57, the Python interface (UT-10, `MOD-11`). Every finding is valid. Each is fixed at its cause, and the findings that share a cause are fixed by one rule (W2 to W6). The guide's prose for UT-02, UT-05 and UT-06 goes to documentation increment 4, which already revises the guide against both logs | The second amendment's ruling round is this record; the Phase 4 plan's fix entries are rewritten in the same commit. WP56 takes the alpha tag after WP55's | The Phase 4 plan and its record, in this commit |
| W2 | **A command's text output is a reading for a person, and `--json` is the record** (UT-03, UT-12). `nanopnp inspect` reads a record by its schema: a run gives its model, its stages, each rung with its Newton iterations and time, the quantities and the deviations; a sweep dataset gives a table of its points and each rectification ratio beside the values that make its pair; a stored artefact gives its schema, key and summary. `--json` prints the whole record, unchanged. Two runs given together are compared: the case fields that differ, the deviations of each, and each quantity side by side | Information that a person asks for is no longer found by reading JSON. A caller that parses `--json` sees no change. FR-34's comparison in the workbench is not moved | IF-02's NOTE on reading, in this commit |
| W3 | **The figure half of FR-28 is brought to v0.5 as FR-44** (UT-01, UT-04): `nanopnp plot` draws the figure of a stage of a case, of a completed run, or of a sweep's dataset to a PNG, SVG or PDF file, through FR-30's registry and its optional extra. FR-28 keeps the export of fields and of the case file, at v0.7 | A mesh and a solved field can be seen from the command line. No layer gains an import of `plot/`: the CLI draws through `core.stages.draw`. P2's and P4's sentences on FR-28 are amended by this ruling | FR-28, FR-44 and Appendix A, in this commit |
| W4 | **The controls that the walk offers reach the shells that run it** (UT-07, UT-10). `nanopnp run` and `nanopnp stage` report each stage and each continuation rung on standard error as they start, on by default where standard error is a terminal; `Case.run` passes on `progress`, `cancel` and the hooks of `run_document` (WP57) | A long solve is no longer silent. The hooks are not inputs (VER-44), so no artefact key moves | None |
| W5 | **A refusal names a setting that this run reads, and a quantity a document states twice is refused by both its names** (UT-06, UT-02, REV-154). The wall-distance gate names `numerics.mesh.wall_h_nm` for a mesh that stage 6 generates, and the generator of the file for a supplied mesh. Where a field document states its charge twice, in its analytic form and in `q_net_e`, a refusal of their disagreement names both keys, and a disagreement in sign is refused when the document is read | A user who changes what a refusal names changes the run. A flipped sign is refused by name when the document is read, not as a conservation error of the grid. A form's charge is not always `q_net_e` (a ring on the axis integrates to half), so the size stays the producer leg's | QR-12's NOTE on the remedy, in this commit; REV-154 accepted, for WP56 |
| W6 | **What a user needs to drive a command or write a document is where they meet it** (UT-02, UT-05, UT-06, UT-08, UT-11). The guide's prose follows in documentation increment 4 | None |

WP57 plans and delivers it | H2's choice is made: neither name enters `PUBLIC` as it is. REV-145, deferred to Phase 5 by V3, is answered in WP57. REV-155's session 04 variation goes to WP57 | `MOD-11` and REV-145, in this commit; FR-27's NOTE on extension, in WP57 |
