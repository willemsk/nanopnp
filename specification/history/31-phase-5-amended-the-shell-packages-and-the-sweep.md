#### 8.2.31 Phase 5 amended: the shell packages and the sweep, agreed 3 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 3 November 2026, at `/np-plan amend 5`, after WP5.02 merged and took `v0.6.0-alpha.2`. §8.2.28
AD1 left four things to this amendment: the shell packages, the sweep's design round (§8.2.19 U4; REV-139), REV-136's
v0.6 cut line (U3) and the phase estimate. Seven questions were put through `AskUserQuestion` in two calls. The first
held the slicing, the sweep's variables, its rebuild detection and the comparison's difference field; the sweep's
questions were asked over mockup A's round-2 sweep (`st-sweep`), the first take WP5.01 drew. The second held the two
questions the author's choice of expressions raised and the fallback on the 200 ms line. The author chose against the
recommendation twice: variables may be expressions, and units are checked through `pint`. The colleague who observes
QR-10 was not asked again: §8.2.17 S24 names them by the close. The letter series continues at AG.

| AG2 | **A variable in a case is a named value with its unit, or an expression of other variables** (*against the recommendation, which was named values only*). Sweeping a variable moves every field that uses it. The variables and the forecast (AG5) are a library package, **WP5.07**, after WP5.06 and before the shell, taking `v0.6.0-alpha.7` | About two weeks instead of one. | AG3 | **Units are checked through `pint`, a new runtime dependency** (*against the recommendation, which was a small dimension algebra in `core/`*). Each variable declares its unit; a field takes its unit from its name's suffix (`_nm`, `_mV`); a variable of another dimension is refused at the field, and one of the same dimension is converted | WP5.07 vets `pint` (BSD-3-Clause, pure Python, so CON-07 holds) before it adds it, and records its version in the manifest as every dependency's is | FR-39, in this commit; the dependency in WP5.07 |
