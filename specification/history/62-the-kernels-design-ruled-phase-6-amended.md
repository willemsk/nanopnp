#### 8.2.62 The kernel's design ruled, Phase 6 amended, agreed 27 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 27 November 2026, at `/np-plan amend 6`, on branch `ccr-4940d566-rjlh0x`, after WP6.09
merged (PR #224; `v0.7.0-alpha.9`). The session read [the kernel's design](../../docs/design/kernel.md), the Phase 6
plan, the open rows REV-67, REV-298 and REV-300, MOD-10 and the measured cost of the three carried packages. It
checked two things in the tree before it asked. `tests/tier2/data/number_stability.json` holds no walk that takes
PHY-23's switch with a stabilised mode, and no cell Péclet number, so BL5 moves no stored number. VER-17's test,
`tests/tier2/test_access_conductance.py`, solves `pnp`, with no flow. The questions went through `AskUserQuestion`
in four calls, each with its options and previews. The session described the 3D benchmark's second option wrongly
in the first call, as VER-17 "with flow", said so, and asked again; BL4 is the answer to the corrected question.
On the backend's seam the author asked "Which one of the two options is more general one? Can you explain both
options in plain language?", and the session explained, changed its recommendation and asked again. The session
recommended against the design page three times: in BL3, in BL5 and, after the author's question, in BL6. The author chose the recommendation in seven rulings
and against it in BL4 and BL10; BL1 is in the author's words. The letter series continues at BL.
