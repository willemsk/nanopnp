#### 8.2.47 Phase 5 amended: the early close, agreed 21 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 21 November 2026, at the seventh `/np-plan amend 5`, on branch
`claude/adoring-albattani-iw6y0g`, after WP5.22's design round and its verdicts. The author had ruled the same day that
the restructure the [architecture survey](../../docs/project/architecture-survey.md) proposes opens Phase 6 and that
Phase 5 closes as soon as it can, its remaining packages reviewed so that nothing is built twice (REV-272). This round
placed each move. Six questions went through `AskUserQuestion` in two calls, the package sequence first, each with its
context, options and previews. The author chose the recommendation four times, ruled AW2 in a free answer, and chose
against the recommendation at AW5. The amendment's review, the same day, put two consequences of AW1 to the author
in one further call, AW7 and AW8; the author chose the recommendation in both. WP5.27's plan, the same day, checked rows
Q01 to Q41 against the browser shell's code and put what it found to the author in one call, AW9 to AW11; the author
chose the recommendation in each. The letter series continues at AW.
