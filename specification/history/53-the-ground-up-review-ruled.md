#### 8.2.53 The ground-up review ruled, agreed 24 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 24 November 2026, at `/np-plan project`, on branch `claude/wp-6-04-findings-review-ada48p`.
This is the round that §8.2.51 BA11 and BA13 placed after WP6.04: the ground-up review was merged (pull request
213) and its 25 differences, `GU-01` to `GU-25` of [the report](../../docs/project/ground-up-review.md), waited as
REV-280. It is a rescoping round, since BA12 let the proposals move requirements and phases. The versions agreed
before it but for two points, both corrected here or named: `docs/plans/current.md` still read "in ship" for
WP6.04, and the tag `v0.7.0-alpha.4` was not yet published, which is the author's to push. The round read the
report, its challenge and its walk's rows that are not plain keeps, §8.2.51 and §8.2.52, §2.7, §8.1, §8.3, the
Phase 6 plan, the input note and REV-280 to REV-287. Twenty-six questions went through `AskUserQuestion` in nine
calls, each with its context, options and previews, the ones that change the phases first. The author chose the
recommendation sixteen times and against it nine times: in BC1, BC3, BC4, BC6, BC15, BC21, BC22 and BC24, and in
asking that the eight proposals the report left to later rounds be put one by one, where the session recommended
carrying them. BC10 began as a free answer. One fact behind BC3 was taken from the report and the physics reviewer and not
checked at its source in the round: that VER-62's stored numbers cover the default switches alone; the package that
stores the guard numbers checks it first. The places of REV-281, REV-282, REV-285 and REV-286 below are the
session's reading and were not asked. The letter series continues at BC.
