#### 8.2.43 Phase 5 amended: the meso suite and the performance package placed, agreed 18 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 18 November 2026, at the fifth `/np-plan amend 5`, on branch `claude/serene-pascal-dmnl60`,
after WP5.19's merge as `v0.6.0-alpha.19`. At WP5.19's item 13 the author had ruled a bridge suite between Molecular and
Continuum, in a package of its own (§8.2.42 AR8; WB-43; REV-259), and the 200 ms redraw's measurement in a dedicated
performance package (AR7; REV-258), each placed by `/np-plan`. The author opened this amendment with "Introduce a WP
for the meso suite." The session put three questions through `AskUserQuestion`, each with what it moves and the
recommendation first: the package's place, the performance package's place, and the suite's name. A fourth followed,
on how far the renumbering the first two make reaches, since AL26 had allowed the records to be edited "for this one".
The answers are recorded as given. The letter series continues at AS.
