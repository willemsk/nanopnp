#### 8.2.22 Phase 4 amended: the geometry split and WP57 widened, agreed 30 October 2026

Rulings by the author at `/np-plan amend 4` on 30 October 2026, taken after WP56's review while its
PR was open. The author asked to rule that UT-02's geometry and meshing be split, ideally in WP57,
to fold the open review items into that package, and to be asked first. Eleven questions were put
through `AskUserQuestion` in three calls. The author took the recommendation on the open items
(adding REV-111 and REV-153), the old commands, REV-144, VER-62, the plan cap and REV-111. The
author chose otherwise on the split's level (both, X1), the packaging (all in WP57, X5), the branch
(X10), the rectification sentence (X8) and the estimate (X9). The letter series continues at X.

| # | Decision | Consequence | Clause changed, and when |
|---|---|---|---|
| X1 | **An idealised pore is geometry the case declares, and meshing is stage 6's** (UT-02). A case declares a cylindrical pore as `geometry.pore`, its membrane and reservoir taken from `geometry.membrane` and `geometry.reservoir`; stage 5 builds the region from it and stage 6 meshes it under `numerics.mesh.*`, as it meshes a structure's region. Beside it, `nanopnp geometry cylinder` writes the region as a standalone geometry file, which `nanopnp plot` draws and `nanopnp mesh` meshes to an MSH 4.1 file under `--maxh-nm` and `--wall-h-nm` | Geometry and its discretisation are separate for every pore, and the mesh settings of a cylinder run are read from the case and recorded in its manifest.
