#### 8.2.51 The project rescoped, agreed 23 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 23 November 2026, at `/np-plan project`, on branch `ccr-a184c192-2cslg6`. This is the
open-minded rescoping round that §8.2.50 AZ7 placed after the cleanup: WP6.03 was merged (pull request 209) and the tag
`copilot-host` pushed. The versions agreed before it: §2.7, §3, §8.1, `CHANGELOG.md` and the published tags all gave
v0.7 for Phase 6, with `v0.7.0-alpha.3` the last tag; `docs/plans/current.md` still named `v0.7.0-alpha.2` and WP6.03's
review as the next step, and is corrected in this commit. The round read §3 in full, each row of §8.1, OPN-08, the
outdated list of WP6.02's Outcome, the architecture survey and Phase 5's end-of-phase report. Its brief told the author
that every requirement not yet built was asked about, and that what was delivered through v0.6 and the exclusions of
§3.5 were not. Thirteen questions went through `AskUserQuestion` in five calls, each with its context, options and
previews, the ones that change the phase sequence first. The author chose the recommendation five times, chose against
it seven times (BA1, BA3 to BA7 and BA12), and gave BA10 as a free answer when asked whether anything else was to be
kept, cut or added. The round's review (code review 079) put two findings to the author the same day, in one call of two
questions: BA14 and BA15, both chosen against the recommendation. The letter series continues at BA.
