#### 8.2.34 Phase 5 amended: the frame on Quasar's components, the physics model and the design loop, agreed 5 November 2026

Ruling by the author on 5 November 2026, at `/np-plan amend 5`, after WP5.08's merge and the author's verdicts on its
frame (`docs/design/reviews/2026-11-05-frame.md`; REV-211 to REV-215). The author asked for "a lot more to discuss
regarding the functionality and design of the GUI", "with only long-term viable solutions and re-use of existing
components/templates/frameworks where possible", and to explore the `impeccable` skill, which "might clash with the local
`np-gui` workflow". The session read the verdicts, the frame's code and the plugin, ran the plugin's detector on a
deliberately faulty page (seven findings) and on the frame and the mockups (none), and put four rounds through
`AskUserQuestion`. The author answered the physics model's contents in their own words, which the second round put back
against S3 and S7. The rulings were played back in one message and the author confirmed them: "I confirm." The letter
series continues at AJ.

| # | Decision | Consequence | Clause changed, and when |
|---|---|---|---|
| AJ1 | **The frame is rebuilt on the components NiceGUI ships through Quasar before any suite is built.** The application layout with its header, side drawers and footer, the tree, dense tables, the searchable select with keyboard selection, the icon font, NiceGUI's sub-pages so that a suite changes without a reload, and the colour tokens fed into Quasar's own colour variables so every control takes the theme. The author chose this over fixing the frame in each suite package and over rebuilding only the marked screens. NiceGUI and Mol* stand (AF4) | The hand-written layout and most of its CSS retire; REV-203's landmarks and keyboard moves, REV-207, REV-208, REV-210 and REV-211 close in that package, and each suite reuses the parts. About a week | The design document's WB-33, in this commit; open: WP5.10 |
| AJ2 | **Physics is a third reusable model of the project's library, built in a Physics suite; the five suites show as those that build models, Molecular, Continuum and Physics, and those that use them, Study and Analysis.** Asked how the suites that build components should stand apart from those that use them (REV-213), the author chose a physics model of its own over two groups of four suites, a Physics suite editing the continuum model, and one model tree; then five suites in two groups over four with physics inside Continuum. A study pairs one model of each kind | Continuum keeps the protein geometry, the region and the mesh; the case gains `models.physics` and a physics model's schema, and a project folder `models/physics/`. WB-02, WB-11, WB-16 and WB-29 are amended | FR-31, FR-32, §8.1's Phase 5 row and the design document's WB-34, in this commit; §5.3.1's NOTE on `models:`, §5.3.2's project folder and §5.3.3, open: WP5.09 |
| AJ3 | **The physics model holds the equations, chosen from the registry, their switches, the electrolyte with its corrections, the boundary conditions, the solid permittivities, and the element orders and stabilisation.** The study keeps the Newton, continuation and linear-solver settings. Asked which values move, the author: "I think the physics suite should allow you to build/edit the actual equations, the effectively build the physics part. It should then allow for exposing certain relevant knobs and parameters to the other suites (like concentrations, bias voltage, etc). Kind of like in COMSOL, but with an extra "interface" layer to the other suites." Asked then where the discretisation belongs, the author placed it with the physics | A physics model fully defines its discrete equations and is reused across pores; the bias and the walls are its defaults, which a study overrides. VER-62's validated defaults change owner and no key moves | FR-39, in this commit; §5.3.1's NOTE on `models:`, open: WP5.09 |
| AJ4 | **Composing equations stays the composer's own phase, and opens inside the Physics suite.** The author's answer in AJ3 asks for the composer (FR-40), which S3 builds from registered blocks and S7 places after v0.6 so that validation is not held behind it; put back with both quoted, the author chose the suite now and the composer next over the composer in Phase 5 and typed equations. v0.6's Physics suite chooses among the registered models, and draws the composer's place with its later-phase banner | S3, S7 and FR-40 stand; the physics model's file and its exposed values do not change when the composer lands | None; the design document's WB-34, in this commit |
| AJ5 | **Every model declares the values it exposes, each with its name, unit, range and default; a study lists those first and sweeps them directly, and any other value stays reachable as reaching inside the model.** The author's "interface" layer of AJ3, put as a question, chose this over a study overriding only the exposed values. AG12 stands | Concentration and bias on a physics model, pH on a molecular model, the mesh's size scale on a continuum model; the units are WP5.07's | FR-39 and the design document's WB-35, in this commit; open: WP5.09 (the declaration), WP5.14 (the Study suite) |
| AJ6 | **`impeccable` is a tool inside the `np-gui` workflow, never its authority.** `np-gui`'s review adds the plugin's critique beside the GUI reviewer's, and a session reads its quality floor for working applications before it builds a screen; a design file generated from `docs/design/tokens.css`, marked as derived, lets its checks read the tokens. The design document and the tokens stay the authority, the rulings win over its rules (it flags Inter, ruled by WB-21), and a host without the plugin skips the step. The author chose this over the plugin leading the design rounds and over copying its checklists | One authority and one record of rulings; about a day | §8.4.4, in this commit; `.claude/skills/np-gui/`, open: WP5.10 |
| AJ7 | **A changed value is marked at its field, filtered on its pane and listed in one dense table, and the project's name heads the project rail.** Every tool pane and the settings filter to modified values; the changed count opens a table of rows about 26 px high grouped by model and study, each with its value, default, unit and reset, and jumping to its field. The name moves out of the menu row to the head of the rail, as the tree's root, and into the browser tab's title. The author, who asked how comparable tools do it (REV-214), chose this over a filter alone and a diff against the defaults | FR-34's list keeps its place as the run's switches (FR-25), denser | FR-34 and the design document's WB-36, in this commit; open: WP5.10 |
| AJ8 | **Each suite is designed on a working prototype over 2WCD, then built.** A suite package opens with its tools prototyped over 2WCD's artefacts and rendered in their states (empty, building, built, refused) for the author's verdicts, then a design round on what stays open, then the tools built, tested and reviewed. The render set gains a 2WCD state, walked once and cached; Physics, with no molecule, uses the KCl case. The author chose this over HTML mockups first and over building then reviewing (REV-215) | About a day or two per suite package; the charge screen's design is settled in the Study package's round | §8.4.4 and the design document's WB-38, in this commit; open: WP5.10 (the state), WP5.11 to WP5.15 |
| AJ9 | **The settings are one declared list.** Each setting is declared once with its section, its scope, the user's or the project's, which overrides the user's where both are allowed, its default, its description and search words, and when it applies; the settings page with a searchable section list beside the panel, the palette's setting commands and both files follow from the list. Nothing that can change a result is a setting. The sections are appearance and accessibility, keyboard and editing guards, runs and storage, and units and numbers. The author added: "Compute settings, network settings, ... I think we need to do some research here." | AI13's per-user rule is amended: a project may carry settings beside its layout. REV-204's guards follow the page | The design document's WB-37, in this commit; the settings' list and the research, open: WP5.10 |
