#### 8.2.16 Phase 4 amended: the specification and the workflow restructured, agreed 22 October 2026

Rulings by the author, taken through `/phase-plan amend 4` after WP51 merged and before the protocol
started. The author asked for two packages before the protocol. The first would streamline the skills
and the use of agents, and redistribute the specification, which "takes a lot of context when read in
full". The second would revise the notebooks and figures further, from a document of comments the
author supplies when that package is planned. Measured at `1ca60b8`: `SPECIFICATION.md` held 78,404
words in 4,153 lines, of which the §8.2 rulings took 14,550 in 119 rows, and the repository named the
file 364 times. The Phase 4 plan held 15,397 words. The skills a session loaded came from the primary
checkout, which was 186 commits behind `origin/main`. The letter series continues at R.
