#### 8.2.39 WP5.16's plan: the pore axis, the Prepare tool's view and variants, agreed 11 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 11 November 2026, at `/np-plan wp 5.16`, after WP5.15's merge. Three questions went
through `AskUserQuestion`: how a pore that is one chain, or built from different chains, is declared so that stage 1
finds its axis (REV-121; §8.2.24 Z5); how the Prepare tool moves the axis and the membrane in its 3D view (WB-14); and
what a variant saved by the Charge edit tool is in the project (FR-38; WB-04). The author answered the first with all
three options offered, so a fourth question put the combination back, with the chain groups' cost, and the author
confirmed it. The letter series continues at AO.

| # | Decision | Consequence | Clause changed, and when |
|---|---|---|---|
| AO1 | **A pore's axis is found whatever the pore, all in WP5.16.** `symmetry.axis: auto` takes the axis from the ring of chains, or of chain groups such as `A+B,C+D,…` that make each group one repeating unit, under C2 and above, and from the channel's empty space under C1, which takes every listed chain whatever their number or kind; `axis: cavity` asks for the channel's axis on any pore, with no rule matching the chains; `axis: z` is checked against whichever axis applies. Offered the channel's axis under C1, a new value `cavity`, or chain groups for a mixed pore with a repeating unit, the author answered: "How about all of these options? Support for a pore such as PlyAB will require multi-chain support." The combination was put back with its cost, about one more session and two agent hours, and the author chose it in WP5.16 (*the recommendation*) over the groups in a package of their own and over leaving out `cavity` | A monomeric pore and a mixed pore with no repeating unit run with a checked axis; PlyAB keeps the ring's spacing and turn checks over its eight units. Groups are written in `source.chains` and `cavity` is a value of `symmetry.axis`, so no key of an existing case moves. The channel's axis carries its own gates and its own displacement budget for `z`, measured and argued in §5.2's NOTE on stage 1 | open: WP5.16 (§5.2's NOTE on stage 1, §5.3.1's NOTE on `structure:`, VER-48); the Phase 5 plan's WP5.16 entry, in this commit |
| AO2 | **The Prepare tool draws the pore's axis and the membrane in its 3D view from the values typed in its pane and stepped by the arrow keys; dragging them by handles comes later.** The author chose this (*the recommendation*) over handles dragged in WP5.16, which need pointer code over a viewer that ships unmodified (CON-09) | The keyboard path that WB-24 asks for comes first; dragging is REV-238, carried to the keyboard and canvas pass of WP5.21 or a later tool package | The design document's Prepare row, in this commit; REV-238 |
| AO3 | **A variant is linked to its parent by default: its own item, holding only its residue edits and reading every other value from its parent, so a change to the parent reaches it; a full copy, naming its parent and changed independently, is offered beside it.** The author: "Default as linked, with the option to go full-copy as well." (*the recommendation*, with the copy added), over a copy alone and over the edits kept in the model itself | A linked variant's run records the current parent as its unedited table (FR-38's `variant_of`), never a stale copy. A model file gains `variant_of`; a linked variant's parent is no variant and holds no edits. The changed count shows a variant's difference from its parent (AG11) | The design document's Charge edit row and *Organisation*, in this commit; open: WP5.16 (§5.3.1's NOTE on model files) |
