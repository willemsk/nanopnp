#### 8.2.11 Phase 4 amended: the development loop simplified, agreed 13 October 2026

Rulings by the author, taken through `/phase-plan amend 4` after WP43 merged. The evidence is WP43's
own record and the usage of its sessions. WP43's commit hook measured 136 to 234 s against the
3.5 minutes it replaced (target 60 s), its push gate 555 s against about 570 s (target 300 s), and
only its pull-request leg met its budget (225 s). Its implementation session cost $64 against
$11 to $19 for earlier ones, with 578k output tokens against 110k to 220k; its shipping session
cost the same as WP42's. About 6,650 lines were added, of them about 2,700 of selection and
budget machinery and tests for it. In `src/`, 32 % of lines are docstrings, and in `tests/` 18 %.
Package numbers in §8.2.7 to §8.2.10 are those at the time of the ruling; from L1 the small fixes
are WP46, the OKF bundle WP47, its backfill WP48, the protocol WP49 and the fixes from WP50.
