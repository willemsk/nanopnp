#### 8.2.44 WP5.20's plan: the atoms an edit moves, and the overlay drafted, agreed 19 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 19 November 2026, at `/np-plan wp 5.20`, after the fifth amendment's merge (§8.2.43). The
survey found that the design's link from an edited outline to "the atoms it cuts" (WB-43) left open which atoms those
are, and that REV-259 put REV-240's overlay to the suite's round without saying which package builds it if kept. Two
questions went through `AskUserQuestion` in one call, each with its context, options and previews, after a brief of
what the plan had settled. The letter series continues at AT.

| # | Decision | Consequence | Clause changed, and when |
|---|---|---|---|
| AT1 | **The Outline tool marks the atoms an edit moves across the outline, each way.** The author chose this (*the recommendation*), over every atom the outline leaves outside it, edited or not, and over the atoms whose sphere the outline's line passes through | An atom of the protonation table, every frame, in stage 1's frame reduced to (r, z), is moved where it lies inside the outline the edit started from and outside the edit, or the other way. Mol* marks those residues in two colours, into the water and into the protein, each named once with its chains' copies (REV-259), and the pane lists each with its atoms counted and its charge per copy. An unedited outline marks none | VER-60's restated NOTE and the design document's Outline row, open: WP5.20 |
| AT2 | **REV-240's overlay is drafted for the meso suite's round and built in WP5.20 if kept.** REV-259 put to that round the overlay of the 3D structure, the full 3D charge and the reduced (r, z) charge turned about the axis. The author chose a working draft at the round, built in this package if kept (*against the recommendation*, which was a sketch at the round and a later package), over dropping it | Charge map draws, over the structure cut at the axis, the deposit's charge density in 3D and its mean about the axis, each as a positive and a negative isosurface from one grid the library computes from the protonation table's atoms; Mol* reads them as MolViewSpec volumes, so no dependency is added. About one to two sessions and two to four agent hours more. If the round drops it, the draft is removed and REV-240's point closes | The Phase 5 plan's WP5.20 entry, in this commit; the design document's Charge map row and REV-240, open: WP5.20 |
