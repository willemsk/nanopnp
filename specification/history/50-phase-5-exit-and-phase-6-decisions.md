#### 8.2.50 Phase 5 exit and Phase 6 decisions, agreed 22 November 2026

Rulings by the author on 22 November 2026, at `/np-plan phase 6`, on branch `claude/loving-allen-ejewkc`. Phase 5 is
closed: its close package is merged, its end-of-phase report is written and `v0.6.0` is published. The versions agreed
before the round: §2.7, §3, §8.1, `CHANGELOG.md`, `current.md` and the published tags all gave `v0.6.0` for Phase 5 and
v0.7 for Phase 6. The round took what §8.2.49 left to it: the packages' numbers and order (AY1, AY6), the placement of
the library's refusals and of REV-270's library part, and §5.4.1's interface with the rest of MOD-13. A survey of the
code came first (a Sonnet 5.5 sub-agent, read against the source). Six questions went through `AskUserQuestion` in two
calls, each with its context, options and previews, the ones that change the package sequence first. While the plan
was being written the author asked whether the open-minded rescoping of the project, remarked on in earlier
discussions, was in it. It was not: the records held only REV-272's round that "re-aligns the scope", taken at §8.2.49
as a re-ordering. A seventh question put its place to the author (AZ7). An eighth, at WP6.01's plan, placed the three-platform check (AZ8). The author chose the recommendation five times
and chose against it at AZ5 and AZ6. The letter series continues at AZ.
