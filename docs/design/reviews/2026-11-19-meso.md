## The charge map and the structure's look

The author's note on Charge map asked for options. Three questions were put at the collect, the third twice:

1. *The map's colour range*: the 2WCD map runs to ±728 C m⁻², a value a few atoms near the axis set, and only 1 % of
   its charged cells pass 175. Asked, the author chose "Clip, and let me change it": the scale opens on the 99th
   percentile of the charge's size, which a *Map range* field changes; a range field opening on the largest value,
   and a logarithmic scale, were declined.
2. *Where the structure's styles are set*: the author chose "On all three Meso screens", Molecular's representations
   in each tool's pane, over Charge map alone or the cartoon alone.
3. *What a picked residue shows*: offered ball and stick, spheres or Mol*'s outline, the author answered: "Solve this
   in a holistic way, offering the ability to change/configure the representations in a flexible way." Asked again
   with two designs, two layers (the structure, and the residues the tool is about) or a free list of layers, each
   choosing what it draws, its style, its colour and its opacity, the author chose "A free list of layers", which
   the option put as a package of its own (REV-265). The *Structure* switch of question 2 is its first layer.
