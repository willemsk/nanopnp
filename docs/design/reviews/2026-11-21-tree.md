# The tree of results: the design round

WP5.22's design round (REV-268; WB-51, WB-53), run by `/np-gui grill` with the author on 21 November 2026 over the
boards of `docs/design/concepts/tree/`, drawn on example 07's 2WCD solved at +50 mV, its bias sweep over ±50 and
±100 mV and its variant with one ASP neutral. The boards were published at
`https://claude.ai/artifact/LiQfF6svDHBWJoT1VmnPsD`, each with a live board to zoom, hover and turn.

**The boards.** Three organisations of the Analysis suite as a tree (T1 COMSOL's branches, T2 one shown run over
tiles of plot groups, T3 one project tree in the rail), the plot types (P1), and the same four plots in Plotly (L1)
and in ECharts (L2). Their data are the solved fields read through the library (`generators/tree/extract.py`):
the fluid's fields on the pore's close-up, the probes, the line along the axis, one I–V point per bias, the
recorded quantities and the library's difference of the two runs (`post/fields.py:field_difference`). Two choices
are the boards' own and differ from WP5.21's explorer: a map draws the field in the fluid alone (the explorer draws
the potential inside the protein too, so its scale is ±155 mV, not ±63 mV), and a one-signed map opens on its 1st
to 99th percentile. The render is `tree-r2` of the tree committed as `0619cc1`: 24 shots, light and dark at
1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800, with no page error or console message; Plotly's own toolbar, shown on L1 alone,
misses the 24 × 24 px target (24 × 22) and its focus ring measures 1.29:1 on dark.

**The rulings.** The round ran by its questions, ten points in the agenda's order, each played back and confirmed
("Confirm all"). The rulings are WB-54 to WB-63 in the design document's decision log. Three went against the
recommendation: Plotly's reach to every (r, z) view, all in WP5.22 (WB-57); one results file per project, after the
author asked whether a file per plot group scales better (WB-60); and a run's label typed by the user (WB-63). The
cards were marked after the round (*The verdicts*, below): they keep T1, P1 and Plotly, and reopen where the settings
stand, how many views show and which runs Data holds (REV-273).

**How they are built.** `/np-plan wp 5.22` plans them: library steps first (REV-270's P2 read; a variable's
evaluation; the probe, arrow and revolution samples; a run's name; the results file and the comparisons read into
it; the default tree from a model's declarations), then the tree, then every chart and (r, z) view moved to
Plotly, with a round of verdicts on the Analysis, Meso, Continuum and Physics screens.

## The verdicts

*prefer* (T1, P1, L1 and today's explorer), two *borrow* (T2, T3) and one *reject* (L2). The notes are quoted as the
author wrote them. They keep COMSOL's branches, the plot types and Plotly, and reopen three rulings: where the
settings stand, how many views show, and which runs Data holds (REV-273). On the same day the author moved the tree,
the restructure the notes call for and the boards' style to Phase 6, and asked for Phase 5 to close soon (REV-272).
