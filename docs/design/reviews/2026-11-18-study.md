## The questions asked at the collect

- **Charge on the mesh.** Asked what the tool is for and which suite holds it, the author answered: "With meso, I
  really meant a new suite that forms a (potentially bidirectional) bridge between the molecular and continuum
  suites. Another tool that could fit there is the contour creation tool. I want you to take a holistic look at the
  suites and their tools, and give me your opinion."

  The look found that each suite follows a scale, except at the join. Molecular builds the atoms and their charges,
  Continuum the region and the mesh, Physics the equations. The step between Molecular and Continuum is split:
  - Continuum › Pore geometry derives the density, the (r, z) reduction and the outline (stages 2 to 4) from a
    molecular model picked in its pane;
  - Study › Charge on the mesh reduces the charge to (r, z) and puts it on the mesh (stage 7).

  Only the charge tool shows the molecule beside its (r, z) drawing. The opinion given was a bridge suite holding
  *Density*, *Outline* and *Charge map*, each pairing Mol* with the (r, z) view, linked both ways. It needs no new kind
  of model. It moves merged tools and needs its own round, so it was recommended as a package of its own. The author
  chose "Yes, in its own package (Recommended)" over building it in this package or giving the tools pair views where
  they stand (WB-43; §8.2.42 AR8; REV-259).
- **Mol*'s view.** Of a side view cut at the axis, a side view of one subunit, or both with a switch, the author chose
  "Both, a toolbar switch" (WB-44).
- **The rung plots.** Of the Runs tool's main view, a window over the frame, or a taller bottom panel, the author
  chose "Runs tool's main view (Recommended)" (WB-45).
- **Expose (REV-252).** Of a list tool in each suite, *Expose* on every field, or no change, the author chose "A list
  tool per suite (Recommended)". Molecular and Continuum each get an Exposed values tool, and the checkbox beside each
  number goes (WB-48).
- **The Physics pane (REV-255).** Of fixing the layout and the words that WP5.18's final review left open now, or
  carrying them to WP5.21, the author chose "Fix them in item 14 (Recommended)".
- **The sweep's view.** Of a table of points in both modes, or a matrix for two values crossed, the author chose "A
  table in both modes (Recommended)" (WB-46).
- **The 200 ms redraw.** The author did not run `redraw.cjs` on a machine with a GPU, and proposed: "I propose we
  postpone this kind of optimization to a dedicated performance optimization work packages." It is recorded as
  §8.2.42 AR7, amending AG7. Measured headless here at item 11, a pick on 2WCD paints its markers in 297 ms (median)
  and 380 ms (p95), and the server's share is 167 / 243 ms of that. The study's walk is keyed about 45 ms at a time,
  three or four times per pick: the performance package's first fix (REV-258).
