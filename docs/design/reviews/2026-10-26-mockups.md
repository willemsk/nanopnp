# The whole-workbench mockups: the review, round 1

The two whole workbenches of `docs/design/mockups/a/` and `b/`, assembled from the author's picks on the choice
panels (`2026-10-25-choices.md`, *The picks*; WP5.01 item 12). Each draws one working session on 2WCD through the
four suites (WB-29) and the guided route, 32 screens each. The GUI reviewer read the first render, 248 shots at
1440 × 900 and 1280 × 800, light and dark; this record gives each finding its disposition. The mockups were then
redrawn and rendered again (256 shots, no page error, no failed contrast or target check) and published for the
author's verdicts at `https://claude.ai/artifact/W4oUSuDkXS61KoBQGxU7ag`: `A`, `B`, `either` or `neither` overall,
`fine` or `change` per screen. The verdicts, collected at item 14, are the second table; the author chose A, and
`docs/design/mockups/a/` is its revision, round 2. Mockup B stays as it was marked.

None of the findings was blocking.
