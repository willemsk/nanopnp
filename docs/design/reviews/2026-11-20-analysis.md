# The Analysis suite on 2WCD: the author's verdicts, round 1

WP5.21's round (§8.2.45 AU1 to AU3; D12). The Analysis suite's two tools, the results explorer and the comparison,
were drawn on a solved 2WCD project: its study at +50 mV, a bias sweep over ±50 and ±100 mV, and a variant with ASP 21
neutral compared with it. Electrolyte's two plots and Runs' convergence were drawn on uPlot beside them. The shots come
from the render `shell-969e763` and, after the review's fixes, `fix2` and `fix3` of the tree committed as `4651541`:
60 shots in 15 picks. They were published as a digest at `https://claude.ai/artifact/8BGqgNF6Uc8BuV56BbkHze`, and the
verdicts were collected on 20 November 2026.

**The verdicts.** The author marked all 15 picks: 8 `keep`, 6 `change`, 1 `drop`. The changes and the drop raised
three questions that were open-ended and costly, which the author asked to discuss: the layout, as a COMSOL-like tree
of results; the chart library; and the workbench's wording. Each was put to the author with options, with three
smaller ones the notes left open: the rebuild, the map's percentile and the 3D view's framing. The rulings are WB-50
to WB-52. REV-264, ruled to this package at WP5.19's ship, was ruled with the recommendation: the solve records its
fields' norms, which a reproduction compares.

**How they are built.** WP5.21's item 14 builds WB-50 and REV-264, then renders and reviews the result. The tree of
results and the charts' library are REV-268's, a design round and a package of their own before WP5.22; the wording
is REV-269's: the author took a rule and sixteen examples at `https://claude.ai/artifact/Aa7jSmQKSrY6enQPZnMACy`,
applied here, and WP5.23 sweeps the rest. What the review left open is REV-267's.

**The columns.** *Stands for* counts the shots a pick's verdict covers (`digest.json` beside the page lists them).
*Reviewer finding* names the finding of the `gui-reviewer`'s review of `shell-969e763`, 1 to 28, and its questions
(a) to (c). Notes are the author's words.
