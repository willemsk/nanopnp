# The frame on Quasar's components: the author's verdicts, round 1

The WP5.15 frame at `48847ea`, from its last render (`shell-r4`, with the dialogs, menus, panels and palette
re-rendered in `shell-r5`): 140 shots, light and dark, in five states, a 2WCD project, the shell's extra
states and a compact configuration. They were published whole at `https://claude.ai/artifact/KsNgB2AgaZDkDNeq3cMUbe`.
At the author's request they went out again as a digest of 20 picks at `https://claude.ai/artifact/UffPP1scwQTVBYTE4XBpa8`.
Each pick stands for a group of near-identical shots and says why it was picked and what to look for. The
verdicts were collected on 11 November 2026.

The author marked all 20 picks, and none of the full set: 10 `keep`, 10 `change`, no `drop`. Four changes are
fixed in WP5.15: the unit written twice, a line between the Build and Use suites with quieter icons and the
fold button at the rail's edge, lines between a menu's groups, and the bottom panel's tabs kept in view as its
rows scroll. One note asked whether the refused item's row
belongs in Problems only; asked, the author kept it (below). The other six ask for a redesign, and each is a
`REV-nn` item for the suites' prototype rounds: REV-237, with the 3D view's notes added to REV-234 and the
dialogs' to REV-235. *Stands for* counts the shots a pick's verdict covers (`digest.json` beside the page lists
them). *Reviewer finding* names the `shell-r3` GUI review's finding, `F1` to `F17`, that the pick shows. Notes
are the author's words.
