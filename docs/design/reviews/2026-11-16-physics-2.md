## The question asked at the collect

- **The fields.** Today a field's message, the ● and "Type a value.", sits under its box. The box's text is drawn
  larger than its label. A field of several boxes, such as the solid permittivities, is a column of separate boxes. The
  field is shared by the Molecular, Continuum and Physics suites, so a change redraws all three. The options were:
  - mockups at WP5.19's round;
  - all three changes built in this package, with a round 3;
  - the text size now and the rest at WP5.19.

  The author chose the mockups at WP5.19's round (REV-256).

REV-250, put to the author at the same collect, changes the Physics tools' switches. While the flow is off, the
dielectric-gradient switch is greyed out, and it is always greyed in `pnp`. The ruling is recorded with PHY-23's NOTE
(`specification/history/2026-11-16-phy23-the-dielectric-terms.md`).
