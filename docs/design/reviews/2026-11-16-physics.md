## The questions asked at the collect

- **The concentration axis.** The electrolyte note asked for matplotlib prototypes outside the GUI. Four axes were
  drawn on example 01's seven curves, each above the wall-distance plot, and shown to the author (`axes-options.png`
  in the session's scratchpad):
  - A: linear, 0 to 6.6 M;
  - B: square-root, c = 0 at the origin;
  - C: log, the c → 0 value as a dot at 1 mM;
  - D: linear to 10 mM and log above.

  The author chose B: "B. is fine, but the labels near the origin overlap. Perhaps this is something we make tunable
  later on, once we build/borrow a more general plotting module for the package. To revisit with the analysis suite."
  Item 10 builds B and keeps the labels near the origin apart. REV-253 records the tunable axis.
- **Expose.** *Expose* stands beside every number field that holds a value, in all three suites. In Conditions only
  the bias is such a field. Molecular and Continuum have no Exposed values tool, so their lists change there alone. Of
  removing it from Physics alone with the rest discussed, removing it everywhere, or putting it on every number, the
  author chose "Physics only, discuss the rest" (REV-252).
- **ASD-STE100.** The library holds about 230 refusal texts in `pipeline/` and `io/`, shared with the CLI and matched
  by tests. Of the GUI's words now with the library's later, the library's texts rewritten now as well, or the GUI's
  words alone, the author chose "Rewrite library texts now". Item 9 rewrites the refusals of `pipeline/`, `io/` and
  `api/project.py`, keeping each text's rule, value, case path and remedy (FR-35; QR-12's NOTE). The gates' diagnostics
  of the stages (`mesh/`, `geometry/`, `charge/`, `numerics/`, `solve/` and the rest) are REV-254.

## REV-235's and REV-245's points

**REV-235.** The dark pick's card put the frame's chrome: the title bar's count and palette button, the status line
beside the bottom panel's tabs, and the rail's items without a kind glyph or a changed mark. The author kept it with no
note, so these stay as built and leave REV-235. The Physics screens open no project dialog, settings row, menu,
Glossary or log, so those points wait for the Study suite's round (WP5.19).

**REV-245.**
- **Colours.** "Colors are fine" keeps a material's fill and the fluid's one blue. The colour rule above settles the
  building stage's ●: it takes the info colour, not the warning colour.
- **Layout.** *Expose* is ruled above for Physics; its place in Molecular and Continuum, beside the critique's toggle,
  is REV-252. The "Later" banners stay where they are: the Equations card put them, and the author kept the pick.
- **Words.** The changes list's lead, the two wordings of an unset default and the part card's repeated name come
  under item 9's and item 10's ASD-STE100 rule where the Physics suite shows them, and under REV-254 elsewhere. The
  compact list's scroll cue and the glossary links of SICN, gamma, the isolevel and the smoothing wait for WP5.26's
  keyboard and glossary pass.
- **Canvas and computation.** The canvas's labels and keys, and the domains read from the artefact, stay with REV-245
  as it says.
