# The choice panels: the review, round 1

The choice panels of `docs/design/choices/`, two variants A and B of each open choice of the workbench, drawn after
the author's comment on the style gallery of 25 October 2026: "perhaps next panels could start with A/B choices +
optional comments, followed by an A/B round of 'full GUI' mockups" (WP5.01 item 10). The GUI reviewer read the first
render, eighteen choices at 960 × 640, light and dark; this record gives each finding its disposition. The panels
were then redrawn and rendered again (86 shots, no page error, no failed contrast or target check) and published for
the author's picks, `A`, `B`, `either` or `neither`, with a note, at
`https://claude.ai/artifact/FX2W3zrgWv9yRX2HEAJu8e`. The picks, collected on 26 October 2026 at item 12, are below
with the mockup that carries each; the two whole workbenches are `docs/design/mockups/a/` and `b/`.

None of the findings was blocking.

Every choice was marked. Where the pick is A or B, both mockups carry it. Where it is `either`, mockup A takes
variant A and mockup B variant B, unless the note asks for both, when both mockups carry the two together.

## Wrong facts

| Finding | Disposition |
|---|---|
| A1 Mesh quality measures invented | ME2 offers SICN and gamma, marks the 0.3 gate on the histogram and states the verdict in words, inverted elements included |
| A2 "Validated presets" | ME1 A is a size scale around the reference, the only validated setting, and says a coarser scale may fail the gate |
| A3 Element and time estimates | Removed; the element count shows after a build, with the gate's verdict |
| A4 Parts and sizes unlike §5.2.2's table | ME1 B draws the size table's rows (global, pore boundary and domain, reservoir domain and outer boundary, axis) with maximum, minimum and growth; the membrane is sized at its own thickness |
| A5 A uniform lattice for a mesh | Every mesh view draws a graded mesh, fine at the wall, coarse to the reservoir's arc, with the selected or worst elements marked |
| A6 No half-disc reservoir | Every (r, z) view draws the half-disc around the membrane's end, labelled not to scale; the region's dimensions are on it |
| A7 Stage numbers and the membrane in the protein geometry | GE1 names stages 2 to 4 by their numbers and says alignment, stage 1, is done in Prepare; its view draws the protein alone |
| A8 Cis and trans reversed; no flow conditions | Cis grounded and trans biased, as the default case; walls no flux and no slip; the protein's charge said to be a volume charge assembled in the study |
| A9 KCl, which no correction file covers | NaCl throughout, the validated electrolyte; the corrections shown as "as validated" with their list |
| A10 Two independent ion concentrations | PH2 B sets one salt concentration, the ions following it, electroneutral; both variants offer the same models |
| A11 Q_net per chain; no pKa or warnings | Q_net an integer per frame; pKa shown per residue; PROPKA's warnings counted in both variants |
| A12 Search and residue numbers inconsistent | The search is empty; residues other than ASP 64 are placeholders; the strip has ASP at 64 |
| A13 Membrane thickness editable in two suites | Prepare sets the membrane's place (WB-14), Region its thickness and the reservoir (WB-15); each shows the other's value read-only |
| A14 The route's state against the tool list | The route's frames show the continuum model built; the route pairs the models in a study before it runs |
