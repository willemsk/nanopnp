# The concept boards: the author's verdicts and the review, round 1

The four organisation boards of `docs/design/concepts/`, published for the author's verdicts at
`https://claude.ai/artifact/AvMfguwdYb6731NmZvhSRa` on 25 October 2026 (WP5.01 item 7) and reviewed
by the GUI reviewer on the render of the same day (findings B1 to B19). The organisation round ruled a
hybrid in conversation the same day (WB-01), recorded in the design document's decision log; the author's
marks, made later on the gallery, were collected at item 14, a mark contradicting a ruling reopening it. None
contradicts one: the two boards marked `prefer` are the two WB-01 takes its frame from, and the two marked
`borrow` are the two it borrows from. One note adds a rule, WB-32, whose wheel clause the author later
called "not a literal constraint, just an annoyance that we should address in one way or another"
(§8.2.18 T8).
