Workbench concept boardsWP5.01 design round, organisation Four ways to organise the same areas, each drawing the same two jobs. Greyscale on purpose: judge the organisation, not the look.

The areas (§8.2.17 S24): the molecular viewer and editor, the geometry builder, the mesh builder, the physics assigner and the results analyser, with a run area from the verdicts on today's shell. C1 groups them as molecular, continuum, study and results; C2 and C3 show them as flat tabs; C4 is a layer of routes over any of the others and places none.

Job 1, QR-10's guided run: load 2WCD, accept the defaults, set salt and bias, get a conductance and a field picture. Job 2: a charge-level mutation of one residue (S4), compared with its parent. Numbers are placeholders (x.xx); no artefact supplies them.
