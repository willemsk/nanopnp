The tree of results: the round's boards

WP5.22's design round (REV-268; WB-51, WB-53), drawn on 2WCD's solved study at +50 mV, its bias sweep and its variant with one ASP neutral. The data are written by ../../generators/tree/extract.py into data.js.

How the tree is organised

  • T1 · COMSOL's branches: data, definitions, probes and cuts, quantities and plot groups; one group drawn at a time, tables in the bottom panel
  • T2 · One shown run, tiles of plot groups: every group follows the shown run unless pinned; several groups as tiles
  • T3 · One project tree: the results under each study in the project rail; the suite holds the settings
