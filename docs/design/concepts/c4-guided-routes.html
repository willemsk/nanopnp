C4 Guided routesStrengths and weaknesses One route per job, one question per step, over any of the other three boards.

Principle. The job is the unit. A route asks one question per step with the default filled in, and "Open in expert view" leaves for whichever of C1 to C3 is chosen, at the same place. It is a layer, not a whole workbench.

PersonaStrengthsWeaknesses
P1 Method developersRoutes are a quick start and a demonstration; they stay out of the way.Of little use alone: every change beyond a route needs the expert view.
P2 Computational scientistsA route ends in a saved case and run, so it feeds their own work.Sweeps beyond one condition need the expert view.
P3 ExperimentalistsQR-10's guided run, step by step, with nothing to choose but salt and bias.Each new job needs a route written for it; outside them, P3 is in the expert view.

Job 1 is five steps, the shortest of the four boards. Job 2 is a route of four. Live links (S6) are those of the view each step embeds. C4 combines with C1, C2 or C3; it does not replace them.
