C1 Model builderStrengths and weaknesses As in COMSOL: a case tree of nodes, a settings pane, one graphics window.

Principle. The case is a tree whose nodes follow the pipeline, grouped as the author's three parts (molecular, continuum, analysis); each node has one settings form and one picture. The areas are branches of the tree, not separate windows.

PersonaStrengthsWeaknesses
P1 Method developersEvery setting is one click from the tree; changed nodes are marked, so FR-25's switches stay visible. Familiar to COMSOL users.As drawn, one graphics window: the molecule, the charge map and a field are not seen together (COMSOL allows more). Scripting sits outside the tree; numerics and outputs have no node.
P2 Computational scientistsThe tree follows the case file's main sections; duplicating a case is a natural variant.Comparisons and sweeps are bolted on as extra nodes; many cases make a long tree.
P3 ExperimentalistsWalking the tree top to bottom is a route of its own.Eight nodes before a result; nothing says which ones matter. The tree is jargon until learnt.

Job 1 takes about ten clicks, most on nodes left at their defaults. Job 2 needs a duplicate, a node edit and a comparison node: easy once the tree is known. Live links (S6) work within one node: table and map yes, a residue and a field no.
