Charge on the mesh · 2WCD, pH 7.5 on the reference mesh

What the solve sees of the protein's charge: the molecular model's charges, deposited as a volume charge on the continuum model's mesh (stage 7). The study builds it once for each pair of models, before its first run, and every run reuses it. Check it here before a long sweep: the total must match the model's Qnet, and a residue's charge must land where the residue is.
◉ ASP 64
