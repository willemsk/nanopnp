Workbench choice panels

21 open choices of the workbench, each drawn twice, A and B, on the same placeholder data (x.xx: no artefact supplies the numbers). What the design round ruled (WB-01 to WB-28 in the design document) is not offered again: the suites, the project browser and the run queue always on screen, the library of models paired in studies, the changed-from-default diamond, the tokens. Pick A, B, either or neither for each, with a note if you like; the picks are assembled into two whole workbenches to choose between. A dashed outline marks the part each choice is about; the rest of the screen is context. Parts of a later tool phase carry a banner and no working control.
