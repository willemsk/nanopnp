**Facts established that the knowledge base should hold**
- The solver's reference length is the 1 nm mesh unit (`MESH_LENGTH_UNIT_NM = 1.0`, src/nanopnp/physics/coefficients.py:24). Any other length is refused. NUM-09's `L₀ = a` is not what runs.
- `Pe = ε V_T²/(η D₀)` is 0.3847 at ε_r = 78.15 and η = 0.890 mPa s (tests/tier1/test_scaling.py:35). 0.386 holds only at ε_r = 78.4.
- `Re = ϱ ε V_T²/η²` is 5.74 × 10⁻⁴ with ϱ⁰ = 997 kg m⁻³, ε_r = 78.15 and η⁰ = 8.904 × 10⁻⁴ Pa s.
- With the governing COMSOL coefficients the Na⁺ diffusivity fit at 5.3 M is 8.113 × 10⁻¹⁰ m² s⁻¹. The 8.13 × 10⁻¹⁰ in the errata comes from the thesis's rounded coefficients.
- `η⁰/η^w(0) = 0.379` and `η⁰/η^w(1.45 nm) = 0.988`. The thesis prints 0.37 and 0.99.
- Above 5.3 M the code clamps each concentration and evaluates the fit at 5.3 M. The `cap_above_validity` values are regression targets checked at 5 × 10⁻³ (tests/tier1/test_corrections.py:185-212). `mu0` is a regression target only; μ is derived from D⁰.
- A case that leaves out `electrolyte.corrections` resolves every correction and the steric flux to `none`, and `physics.model` defaults to `epnp-ns`. The validated defaults (`willems2020_nacl`, `borukhov`) live only in src/nanopnp/io/defaults.py:126-176.
- The NUM-17 potential-increment cap is enforced as a damping cut. It aborts only when the cap is still exceeded at minimum damping (src/nanopnp/numerics/newton.py:254-260).
- The Newton defaults beyond NUM-16's table are growth factor 2.0 (the project's own choice), absolute residual floor 10⁻¹² and field-floor ratio 10⁻⁶ (newton.py:30-38). The solver returns before the first step only when the entry residual is at most 10⁻¹².
- `clause.py` gathers NOTEs by the identifiers they name, not by position. 32 of the 33 NOTEs in §6 and 10 of the 16 in §4 do not name the clause they follow.
- The rulings that made the unsmoothed distance field the validated default and that discharge NUM-10 through the direct solvers' own row scaling are recorded only in docs/code_reviews/CODE_REVIEW_010.md:36,52.
