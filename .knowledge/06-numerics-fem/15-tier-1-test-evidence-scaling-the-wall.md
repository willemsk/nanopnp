## Tier-1 test evidence: scaling, the wall-distance clamp and the warm-start rationale (WP45 item 24)

- **[tested]** NUM-09's Péclet number is quoted as 0.386, which needs `ε_r` near 78.4. The model's own fitted `ε_r,f0 = 78.15` gives `Pe = 0.3847`, 0.3 % lower, with no physical effect; the value is pinned rather than rediscovered. At 20 °C and `η = 1.00 mPa s` the frequently quoted 0.34 is the same ratio at the other viscosity temperature, so the two are never mixed across material properties. Source: `tests/tier1/test_scaling.py`.
- **[tested]** The force scale `F_0 = p_0 a² = ε V_T²` is 0.4568 pN, so the NUM-29 tolerance of 0.1 pN is 0.219 `F_0`. `p_0 a²` does not depend on the reference length `a`, so 0.1 pN means the same on a 2 nm pore and a 20 nm one. Source: `tests/tier1/test_scaling.py`.
- **[tested]** A manifest that omits the viscosity cannot say whether a run took it at 20 °C or 25 °C, which is a silent per-cent error in `u_0`, `p_0` and `Pe`. Source: `tests/tier1/test_scaling.py`.
- **[verified]** The viscosity wall form `f^w_η(0) = 1 + exp(−3.36 · (−0.147))` has no root and cannot be inverted: it is 17.7 times wrong at the measured minimum of `d` on the toy pore, which is why the PHY-02 clamp sits on the shared driver and not in the ion form. Source: `tests/tier1/test_wall_distance.py`.
- **[tested]** The ion wall function's slope at the wall is `P1 exp(−P1 P2) = 5.83` per nm, so `d = 10⁻⁹ nm` lies at `10⁻⁷` of the wall value. A `10⁻⁶` tolerance bounds that and still catches a step, which is the discontinuity a clamp on the factor would introduce. Source: `tests/tier1/test_wall_distance.py`.
- **[tested]** On the toy pore (2 nm radius, 30 nm reservoir, 0.15 nm wall) the normalised facet jump of the mollified `grad d` is 0.494. `JUMP_TOL` was measured on a cylinder at 0.1 nm wall resolution, so the two figures are not comparable; the file round trip is held to floating-point equality, which is sharper than any threshold. Source: `tests/tier1/test_wall_distance.py` (`test_num31_the_gradient_jump_survives_the_file_round_trip`).
- **[tested]** The NUM-34 threshold lies between the ion function's root at `−P2 = −0.01 nm` and the element-wise projection residual that `Set` leaves behind (roundoff of order `10⁻¹⁶` at the wall, a few `10⁻⁴` unzeroed). A threshold at the root would admit a diffusivity of exactly zero as its last passing state; a threshold at zero would gate the rounding mode. Source: `tests/tier1/test_wall_distance.py`.
- **[tested]** A distance field re-solved on the same build matches the stored one to ten digits, so agreement cannot show that a restore reads the stored vector; restore tests therefore move the stored vector and require the moved field. Source: `tests/tier1/test_solution_state.py`, `tests/tier1/test_warm_start_descriptor.py`.
- **[tested]** The stabilisation terms vanish identically at `φ̃ = 0`, `c̃_i = 1`, `ũ = 0`, `p̃ = 0` (no wind, symmetric salt at bulk), so every mode is identical at the entry rung of the NUM-18 ladder. Source: `tests/tier1/test_stabilisation.py`.
- **[tested]** The linearisation at zero wind is singular without a guard. NGSolve evaluates `d/du √(g·g)` as `(g · ∂g/∂u)/√(g·g)`, so at `g = 0` the entry is `0/0`; `MAGNITUDE_FLOOR` inside the root keeps it finite. Without it the reference mode aborts at the first linearisation of stage 4 with `UmfpackInverse: Numeric factorization failed`, and nothing in the message names the stabilisation. Source: `tests/tier1/test_stabilisation.py` (`test_ver41_the_linearisation_is_finite_at_the_zero_wind_cold_state`).
- **[verified]** `P1/P1` is not inf-sup stable and `resolve` refuses it outside the reference stabilisation (NUM-03); the warm-start element-order refusal therefore uses P2 against P3. Source: `tests/tier1/test_warm_start_descriptor.py`.
- **[tested]** A rectification pairing must be exact: a tolerance would report 100 mV against −95 mV as a rectification, a ratio between two unrelated operating points. Source: `tests/tier1/test_sweep_plan.py`.
- **[tested]** The §8.3 reference axis carries 17 exactly opposite bias pairs plus one unpaired value at +5 mV, not 0 V: at zero bias the current is zero and the NUM-26 relative route check divides by a vanishing scale. The bias origin is 0 V and the salt origin the low end, so the deepest point is `4 + 17 + 20 = 41` and there are 42 waves. Source: `tests/tier1/test_sweep_plan.py`.
